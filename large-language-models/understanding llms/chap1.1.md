### better representation with dense vector embeddings


bag of words, although a good approach has a significant flaw, it thinks of language as just literally a bag of words, forgetting its semantic nature or the meaning of words in a sentence.


Released in 2013, Word2Vec was one of the first successful attempts at capturing the meaning of text in embeddings.
Embeddings are vector representations of data that attempt to capture its meaning. To do so, Word2Vec learns semantic representations of words by training on vast amounts of textual data, like the entirety of Wikipedia.
